# 🌾 AgriSynk — Integrated Farming Services

> **Connecting CSR funds to farmers. Tracking every rupee. Delivering trusted farm-to-table food.**

AgriSynk is an integrated AgriTech platform designed to connect **farmers, CSR sponsors, corporates, and health-conscious consumers** through a transparent digital farming ecosystem.

The platform brings together **CSR farmer adoption, live fund tracking, farm-level data, organic produce, logistics tracking, and agricultural services** in one system.

---

## 🎯 The Problem

AgriSynk is designed around three interconnected problems:

* 👨‍🌾 Farmers struggle with limited direct market access and dependence on middlemen.
* 💸 CSR contributors lack farmer-level visibility into where funds are deployed and what impact they create.
* 🥗 Consumers looking for trusted organic produce often lack complete farm-to-table traceability and certification visibility.

The platform aims to connect these fragmented systems into one transparent workflow.

---

## 💡 The AgriSynk Solution

AgriSynk uses a **CSR adoption model** where sponsors can support farmers and receive continuous visibility into the farming cycle.

The platform is designed to provide:

* 💰 Transparent CSR fund allocation
* 👨‍🌾 Direct farmer onboarding and support
* 📍 Geo-tagged farm mapping
* 📡 Live fund and logistics tracking
* 🔬 Lab-tested produce
* 📦 Farm-to-door delivery
* 🌱 Organic farming support
* 🚁 Drone and agricultural service booking
* 📊 CSR impact reporting
* ⛓️ Blockchain-backed transaction trails

The proposed model uses a ₹50,000 example contribution to support approximately **4–6 farming families for a Kharif season**, covering inputs, agricultural services, insurance and testing.

---

## 🏗️ Platform Architecture

AgriSynk consists of three primary interfaces:

### 📱 Farmer App

Designed for farmers to:

* Register and maintain farm profiles
* Upload farm and crop information
* Receive fund/tranche updates
* Receive weather and crop advisories
* Track farming activities
* Upload field photographs
* Access services and support

The planned MVP includes a React Native farmer application with Telugu support, Aadhaar OTP, photo uploads and fund alerts.

### 🖥️ Sponsor Dashboard

Sponsors can:

* View adopted farmers
* Track fund deployment
* Monitor farming progress
* View logistics information
* Download CSR reports
* Access farm-level information
* Track impact throughout the farming cycle

### ⚙️ Admin Panel

The administration system manages:

* Farmer onboarding
* Sponsor management
* Fund releases
* Farm verification
* Service providers
* Logistics
* Lab results
* CSR reporting
* Platform operations

## 💰 Business Model

AgriSynk is designed with multiple revenue streams rather than relying on a single source of income.

### Revenue Streams

1. **Platform Transaction Fee** — 5–8% on CSR funds
2. **Agri Services Booking** — 10–15% commission
3. **Food Subscription** — recurring premium/celebrity wellness boxes
4. **Input Supply Margin** — wholesale agricultural inputs
5. **CSR Report Packages** — paid compliance and reporting
6. **FPCL SaaS** — farm-management software subscription

This creates a diversified revenue model across funding, services, food and software.
