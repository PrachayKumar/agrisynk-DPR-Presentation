02 · The Problem
Three Broken Systems. One Opportunity.

Each problem is massive on its own. Together, they create a unique white space that AgriSynk is uniquely positioned to fill.

Farmer Crisis
👨‍🌾

Farmers Earn 30% of What They Grow

4–5 middlemen extract 60–70% of farm produce value. A farmer gets ₹8/kg for chilli that sells at ₹60/kg in Mumbai. 86% of Indian farmers are small/marginal with zero direct market access.

86%
farmers are marginal/small — no direct buyer access
CSR Opacity
💸

₹25,000 Cr CSR With Zero Traceability

India's Section 135 generates ₹25,000 Cr CSR annually. 74% of corporates report difficulty verifying actual impact. Funds go to NGOs with no live tracking, no outcome proof, no farmer-level visibility.

₹25,000Cr
annual CSR — most with zero real-time impact tracking
Food Safety
🥗

83% of Supermarket Produce Fails Safety

CSE 2022 Report: 83% of tested produce has pesticide residue above safe limits. Health-conscious celebrities, athletes, and households have no trusted, verified organic source with traceable farm origin.

of Indian retail produce fails pesticide safety standards
